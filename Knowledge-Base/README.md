# Knowledge base

`knowledge.json` is the knowledge base behind both the hierarchical overview
and the chatbot. It holds 61 entries: four top-level categories and the 57
question-and-answer entries nested under them.

## Categories

| Category (title shown to participants) | Entries | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Getting Started: What is this study about** | 7 | The basics of the study: its objective, who can participate, what data is collected (an anonymized CV and optional sensitive personal information), the general risks and benefits, that participation is voluntary, and ethics approval. |
| **Your Privacy: How we handle your data** | 11 | Data governance: participants' rights, how long data is kept, withdrawal, where the CV and personal information are stored, who the model owner and the trusted third party are, whether data is shared or linked to other datasets, and who anonymizes the data. |
| **Making a Difference: Why your donation matters** | 18 | Data use and benefits: what fairness monitoring is and how it works, how the donated data is used, the fairness metrics (with an example for each), why the CV and personal data are needed, the benefits of donating, and how results are reported. |
| **Safety Measures: How we keep your data secure** | 21 | Risks and technical protections: why the data needs protection, the impact of a breach, and how anonymization, encryption, secret sharing and multi-party computation (MPC) work, including the risks that remain with each. |
